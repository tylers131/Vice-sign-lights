No panel paired

The text panel is a separate Bluetooth device from the twelve light controllers. Power it on, scan, and pick it out of the list.

If nothing here recognises it, run ./matrix_probe.py scan on the Pi -- it says what it found and what to do next.

Say something

Text holds still on this panel. It is drawn one pixel at a time over Bluetooth, and the antenna is shared with the twelve controllers, so moving it would cost the lights more than it is worth — a scroll runs at under two frames a second and never stops. Messages too wide for the panel are shown a page at a time instead.

Event schedule

Shows the day's offerings, tomorrow's hours, the temperature and VICE, and shouts each event while it is on -- all built from the calendar and the clock, updating on its own as the week goes on. Turning this on takes over the panel from the saved queue below.
